What this covers, and what it doesn't

  • Currencies are not converted. Europe is EUR/MWh and the US is USD/MWh, so the two regions' figures are not directly comparable to each other.
  • Day-ahead wholesale energy only — excludes network charges, capacity, ancillary services, levies and taxes.
  • Negative prices are included as-is in the headline figure; the negatives-floored variant is in the table and tooltips.
Europe: European Wholesale Electricity Price Data (hourly), filtered to the latest 12 months. US: NYISO zonal DAM LBMP · ISO-NE day-ahead hourly LMP by load zone · MISO day-ahead ex-post LMP at named trading hubs · CAISO OASIS PRC_LMP at default load aggregation points.
